# Digital Transformation Playbook

A public-safe executive playbook for assessing digital maturity, defining target operating models, prioritizing initiatives, building transformation roadmaps, governing portfolios, driving adoption, and realizing measurable business benefits.

All examples and sample data are fictional.

## Core Areas
- Digital maturity assessment
- Current-state assessment
- Target operating model
- Initiative portfolio
- Prioritization
- Business cases
- Transformation roadmap
- Governance / PMO
- Change management
- Adoption
- Benefits realization
- Risk and dependency management
- Executive reporting

## Lifecycle
Business Strategy → Current State → Maturity Assessment → Target Operating Model → Initiative Backlog → Prioritization → Business Cases → Roadmap → Delivery Governance → Change & Adoption → Benefits Realization → Continuous Improvement

## Design Principles
1. Business outcomes before tools.
2. Every initiative has an accountable owner.
3. Benefits are measurable before funding.
4. Dependencies stay visible.
5. Adoption is a delivery outcome.
6. Maturity guides sequencing.
7. Governance accelerates decisions.
8. Technology maps to process, people, data and controls.
9. Benefits continue after go-live.
10. Public examples use fictional data only.
